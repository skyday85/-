# IMAP onboarding

Add opt-in, read-only IMAP for Mail.ru, Yandex, and Timeweb. Fixed TLS endpoints, separate encrypted gateway-only vault, owner and organization checks, no SMTP permission, no original-message deletion, and deduplication in the shared unified inbox.

A production installation requires a reachable authenticated service and explicit user entry of app passwords in its secured client. No credentials are sent in chat or committed to source control.
